What you'll be doing

As a Tax Consultant at Speedy Tax Return, you will assist clients with their tax filing and related requirements. Your responsibilities will include:

  • Preparing and assisting with individual and business tax returns.
  • Reviewing financial documents and supporting records.
  • Understanding applicable taxation rules and filing requirements.
  • Helping clients understand their tax obligations.
  • Maintaining accurate documentation and client records.
  • Ensuring timely completion of assigned filing activities.

What we're looking for

The ideal candidate will bring a combination of technical knowledge, attention to detail, and a willingness to learn.

  • Graduate degree in Commerce, Accounting, Finance, or a related field.
  • Basic understanding of taxation and accounting principles.
  • Familiarity with income tax filing and related documentation.
  • Good analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills.
  • Ability to work independently as well as within a team.

What you can expect

At Speedy Tax Return, we believe in creating an environment where people can learn, contribute, and grow professionally.

  • An opportunity to develop practical taxation expertise.
  • A supportive and collaborative working environment.
  • Exposure to a variety of client requirements.
  • Opportunities for professional development and learning.
