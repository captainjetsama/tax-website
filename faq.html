How does the tax preparation process work?

Our process is simple and straightforward. First, contact our team and tell us about your tax situation. Next, we'll let you know which documents are needed. Once we receive your information, we prepare your tax return, review it with you, and file it after your approval.

How quickly will I receive my tax refund?

Refund processing times depend on the IRS and your individual tax situation. The IRS generally issues most refunds within 21 days for electronically filed returns with direct deposit. Some returns may take longer due to additional reviews or other factors. We cannot guarantee a specific refund amount or date.

What documents do I need to file my taxes?

The documents you need depend on your financial situation. Common documents include a government-issued photo ID, Social Security information, W-2 forms, 1099 forms, and records of eligible deductions or credits. Our team will provide a more specific document checklist based on your circumstances.

How much do your tax preparation services cost?

Our fees depend on the type of tax return and the complexity of your filing situation. We'll explain the applicable fees before beginning your tax preparation so you know what to expect.

Do I need to visit your office to file my taxes?

No. We offer a convenient remote tax preparation process for clients across the United States. You can communicate with our team and provide the required information without visiting our office.

Can you help self-employed individuals and freelancers?

Yes. We assist freelancers, independent contractors, gig workers, and other self-employed individuals with tax preparation. This may include reporting self-employment income, business expenses, and applicable deductions.

Can you help if I haven't filed taxes in previous years?

Yes. Contact our team to discuss your filing history and outstanding tax returns. We can review your situation and help you understand the available options for getting your tax filings up to date.

Is my personal and financial information confidential?

Yes. We take the privacy and confidentiality of your personal and financial information seriously. Your information is handled in accordance with our privacy practices and applicable requirements. Please review our Privacy Policy for additional details.

Do you provide tax services for businesses?

Yes. We offer services for businesses, including business tax preparation, bookkeeping, tax planning, payroll support, and business-related filing services. Contact us to discuss your business requirements.

How do I get started with Speedy Tax Return?

Getting started is easy. Contact our team by phone or through our contact form. We'll discuss your tax needs, explain the next steps, and help you understand the information required to begin.
